# Code of Conduct

TraceVeil is committed to a respectful, constructive community. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, and sharing another person’s private telemetry or credentials are not acceptable.

Use professional language, assume good intent, and critique ideas and artifacts rather than people. Maintainers may remove comments, contributions, or participants that violate these standards. Report conduct concerns privately to **ateeqdesktop@gmail.com**.
